ADMITTED

02B

Intentionally excluded

NOT ADMITTED
  • FixturesMap machinery and fixture-like combat objects are not objectives.
  • Reward-suppressed summonsSummons explicitly excluded from rewards do not inflate the denominator.
  • Never-triggered encountersEnemies in skipped areas or triggers never spawn and never enter the count.
SPAWN CLEAR ≠ AUTHORED MAP CLEAR

Defeating every enemy that actually spawned can report 100% even when an authored encounter was bypassed. Mission completion remains boss/result-driven and does not require the Defeat All Monsters objective to reach 100%.
